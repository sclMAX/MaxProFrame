Drill Hole pattern: a checkable "Pattern" group with holes on both sides of the picked point, along and across, and an optional hole at the point.
Branch: feat/drill-hole-pattern (worktree ../MaxProFrame-drill-pattern). One PR.

## Specs

- S1. Pattern group (L1, L2): "separa eso en un grupo colapsable". A checkable group "Pattern" (Spanish *Patrón*): unchecked, one hole at the point and the pattern fields hidden; checked, the fields show. Its title sums up the pattern.
- S2. Both sides, both axes (L1-L4): "agregar la posibilidad de que el patron se en una u otra direccion o en ambas". "Las dos cosas (recomendado)": along and across (a grid when both have holes), and on each side of the point. The user's example must work: "<- 3 (cada 20mm) (centro) 5 (cada 30mm) ->", that is 3 holes every 20 mm backward, the hole at the point, 5 holes every 30 mm forward: 9 holes.
- S3. Hole at the point (L5, L6): a "Hole at the point" check box (Spanish *Agujero en el punto*), on by default; off, a centered pair (one hole on each side) has no hole in the middle.
- S4. Compatibility (L5, L6): drill holes saved with Count/Pitch (1.5.0) open with the same holes.
- S5. Expressions: every new number field takes Fx, like the other Drill Hole fields.
