# Stepped corners: Folded Bar joints made only of straight cuts
A per-corner joint type on Folded Bars: `Miter` (today: the inner wall is cut at θ/2, a bevel head is needed) or
`Stepped` (an interlock whose every cut is a straight cut, so any tube laser cuts it). Branch feat/stepped-corners.
Delivery: one PR, commits per task; push, PR and merge stay the user's decisions.

## Specs
Notation (corner frame of unfolded_geometry.py): u along the bar, v across the bent wall from its outside face, w along
the bend axis; d = tube depth across the bent wall, t = wall thickness, θ = bend (deflection) angle, h = θ/2,
s = step depth, g = clearance. A = the tab piece, B = the socket piece.

- S1 Machine-agnostic joint (L2): "Mi maquina no debe ser el patron para el diseño, MaxProFrame debe ser agnostico a la
  maquina (cualquier usuario puede usar nuestro wb independiente de que maquina tenga)." -> no machine setting. Each
  Folded Corner has a joint type `Joint` = `Miter` | `Stepped`. `Miter` is today's geometry, unchanged. Existing
  documents and corners default to `Miter` and recompute to the same shapes.
- S2 Stepped folded corner (L2): "pensar algo similar para las esquinas interiores (un encastre cons los espesores para
  hacer cortes rectos" -> at a folded corner with `Stepped`, the inner wall band v ∈ [d - s, d] is not cut by the
  tilted V flanks. In that band, A's inner wall ends at one tube cross-section (a plane normal to A's axis) and B's at
  one cross-section of B; A covers the whole overlap of the two inner walls, B ends past it. Below the band the side
  walls keep today's V flanks (planes containing w). Every cut surface of the stepped joint is either a tube
  cross-section of the piece it cuts or a plane containing w; none is tilted through the inner wall. The bar folds into
  one sound solid with no overlap between A and B.
- S3 Computed for any angle (L3): "eNCASTRE calculado" -> the step positions are computed for every bend angle MaxProFrame
  folds: A's end is at the farthest point of the overlap along A, B's end at the farthest point of the overlap along
  B (each moved by g/2 away from the other). Results: at θ = 90° A's end face is flush with B's inner face and B's end
  rests on A's visible face over the whole thickness; at θ < 90° a wedge s × s·cot θ opens towards the frame's inside
  between B's end and A's visible face (a weld groove); at θ > 90° no gap shows and A's tip ends hidden inside B.
- S4 Stepped open corner (L2): "algo asi para la esquina exterior (el final del marco)" [Image#2: two pieces cut along
  the miter with a small rectangular step, one with a tab and one with a socket] -> the open corner of a closed frame
  with `Stepped` gets the stepped joint at both ends of the bar, generated by MaxProFrame: the inner wall band and the
  outer wall band v ∈ [0, s] are each stepped as in S2/S3 (A takes the inner overlap, B the outer one, so the corner
  interlocks), the side walls between keep the miter. It replaces the End Miter of the two ends at that corner. With
  `Miter` the open corner keeps today's behavior: its ends keep their own End Miter.
- S5 Step settings (L2, L3): `StepDepth` (length, 0 = automatic = the larger of the wall thickness and the tube's outer
  corner radius, so the tilted flank never crosses the rounded edge of the stepped wall); `StepFlip` (bool, false)
  swaps A and B; the corner's `Clearance` also opens the step faces (g/2 per side). A step that does not fit (band and
  key, or band and relief, overlapping, or s ≥ d / 2) is refused with a message naming the corner's two pieces, like
  the other unfold errors.
- S6 Object and panel (L2): the Folded Corner object carries `Joint`, `StepDepth`, `StepFlip`; older documents get them
  on restore. The Folded Bar panel shows them per corner (combo, length field, check box) with the same multi-select,
  Mixed, expression and remembered-value rules as the other corner fields; the open corner row becomes editable for
  `Joint`, `StepDepth`, `StepFlip` and `Clearance`. Spanish translations, tutorial, README and CHANGELOG describe it.
